# Briefly AI

**Understand more. Remember better.**

Briefly AI is an AI-powered learning and summarization platform that transforms articles, YouTube videos, uploaded videos, and documents into structured, easy-to-understand content.

It helps users save time, extract important information, and turn lengthy content into useful learning resources, all in one place.

## What It Does

- **Content Summarization:** Generate concise summaries from articles, YouTube videos, PDFs, DOCX, TXT files, and uploaded videos.
- **Key Insights:** Extract important concepts, arguments, takeaways, and chapter breakdowns.
- **AI Study Tools:** Turn summarized content into study notes, quizzes, and flashcards.
- **Ask AI:** Ask questions about your content and receive answers grounded in the original sources.
- **Smart Search:** Find relevant information across your saved content using semantic search.
- **Personal Library:** Organize and revisit your processed content whenever you need it.
- **Multiple Summary Styles:** Choose different formats to understand content based on your needs.
